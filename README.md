# Longship
Originally created for the 2026 WWT STEM Student Forum. This solution was designed to improve supply chain efficiency of teachers getting classroom supplies from the bookstore using Google Workspace. We've open sourced our solution for anyone to use.

## Context
 - This system was designed for teachers in the Parkway School District, so the terminology used will reflect that
 - However, you are free to take a copy of Longship and modify it to fit your needs. This is just how it was initially designed
 - Each teacher has a department that they're located in (like math, science, history)
 - Each department has a head teacher, reffered to as the department head. This teacher is the "manager" of the department
 - The bookstore is the warehouse. It's where all the supplies are, and it's where all supply orders go to
 - The system generally works like this: A teacher places an order, the order goes to the department head for approval, and then is sent to the bookstore
 - For a department head, their order is immediately sent to the bookstore

## Info
 - Longship uses Google Apps Scripts to improve supply chain efficiency
 - There is an inventory Google Sheet. This contains inventory data and email authorization (meaning who can actually request supplies)
 - Email authorization includes a list of department heads, as well as the emails and departments of every teacher at the school
 - Any change to the data of the inventory in the Google Sheet is logged in a sheet called "Log"
 - This inventory Google Sheet is connected to an order Google Form. This form is where teachers place their orders for supplies. The form is automatically updated every morning to stay up to date with the inventory data in the inventory Google Sheet
 - Upon placing an order, the department head for the teacher will recieve an email with the order information. They can then reply to the email with "Approved" or "Denied"
 - Approving will send the order to the bookstore. From there, the bookstore repeats this process, where they get an email with the order information and can Approve or Deny the order
 - The bookstore approving an order will automatically reduct the ordered items from inventory and create a log in the "Log" sheet
 - Denying at any point (department or bookstore) will cancel the order
 - The original teacher who ordered items will recieve confirmation emails as their order gets processed or denied
 - Every Monday morning, the highest-in-command will recieve an inventory report generated by Google Gemini, giving a quick summary on inventory data, the items most in demand, and predicting when items will run out

## Instructions (get your own Longship to use)
- Go to the [Longship Google Drive Folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/1/folders/1livWnilfSNy-C8Qe9m4WtmpLISzWBzKF)
- Download the folder onto your computer (it will download as a zip file)
- Upload the zip file back into your own drive
- Add your own inventory data in the "Inventory" Google Sheet
- Change up the emails in the "Inventory" Google Sheet
- And you're done!
